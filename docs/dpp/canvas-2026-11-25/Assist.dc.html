What Assist may and may not do
May
Suggest where scanned papers start and what kind they are. Summarise. Match papers to what the offence needs. Point out gaps and statements that disagree. Draft advice to Police and the wording of a charge from the legal guide.
May not
Decide, sign, send, change a decision, quote a law that is not in the legal guide, or read handwriting and present it as fact.
Always
Shows the page it relied on. Shows its words in grey, marked "Assist suggested", until a named person confirms them. Records that it was used, in the file's history.
Told
The country, the legal guide's text for the offence, the decision set and who may take each, the time limits, this file's papers, and how this office has handled similar files. Nothing else about the law.

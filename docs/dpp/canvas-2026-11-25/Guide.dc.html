Theft
Penal Code Act, sections 254 and 261 · Changed by this office · 12 Nov by D. Nakato
See what changedGo back to the starter text
HOW THE CHARGE IS WORDED
Statement of offence. Theft contrary to sections 254 and 261 of the Penal Code Act.
Particulars. [Accused], on [date] at [place], stole [the item], the property of [owner].
The words in square brackets are filled from the file. Wording follows the layout of a police charge sheet.
WHAT HAS TO BE PROVED
1. A person took the item
2. It belonged to someone else
3. Taken without a claim of right, with intent to keep it
4. The accused is the person
PAPERS USUALLY NEEDED
Statement of the owner · eyewitness or the person who recovered the item · exhibit list, with photograph · accused's statement · proof of value if it matters for sentence
WHAT IS OFTEN MISSING, AND WHAT TO ASK POLICE
No statement from the person who recovered the item: "Please record a statement from the person who recovered it, saying where and when."

How the guide is used
The charge sheet takes its wording from here. The Decide screen shows "what has to be proved" as a checklist against the papers. The advice to Police starts from "what to ask Police for". When Assist is on, it is told exactly this text and nothing else about the law, so it cannot invent a section.
Changes are saved with who made them and when. Nothing is lost: the starter text is always one click away. A change can be marked "waiting for the Director" before the rest of the office sees it.

same words as Pleadli Police
TodayWhat needs you now, and "Continue" for work you left half done.
InboxEverything that arrives: Police files, files returned by Police, court papers, complaints.
FilesThe office's own word for its work, used for trial files too. Papers are the scans and typed documents inside a file.
DiaryCourt days and deadlines. Prosecutors keep a diary and read the court's cause list.
RegistryThe real name of the part that receives, stamps, registers, gives out files and keeps the registers. Supervisors and registry staff only.
SettingsYou, the office, the legal guide, Assist, backup, security.

Three rules for everything
Paper first. Scan or type, by hand, every time. Assist is a side panel and never needed.
One file, every view. Today, Inbox, Diary and Registry only list files; each line opens the same file.
Rules the office can edit. Offences, time limits and wording sit in a plain "Legal guide" that staff keep up to date themselves.

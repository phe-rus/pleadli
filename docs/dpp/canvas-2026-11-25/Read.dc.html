STATEMENT OF WITNESS
I am Akello Mary, a trader at Owino Market, aged 34 years. On Sunday 12 July 2026 at about 4:30 pm I was at my stall when a man I know as John Okello took my phone from the table and ran towards the taxi park. I shouted and Opio David, who sells next to me, ran after him.
I have known the man for about two years because he sells socks near the gate. I saw his face clearly because the market was open and it was daylight. The phone is a black Tecno worth about Shs 450,000.
Later the same day police brought the phone to me at the station and I identified it by the crack on the lower right corner.

# Pleadli Lawyers: first build (2026-09-21)

Written after the fact, alongside the code. The user asked for the Lawyers side to be coded in the same pass as Courts Stages 2 to 7 ("continue coding even lawyer side at once, we will run tests and do everything else once"), which stands in for the separate brief and approval gate that `docs/courts/PROPOSAL.md` (D9) had set for this app. Nothing here was run: no compile, no tests, no window (see "Not run").

Grounding: `docs/lawyers/RESEARCH.md` (the profession, the state-funded scheme, plea bargaining from the defence side) and `docs/courts/PROPOSAL.md` section 7 (what Courts owns and what Lawyers owns). Evidence tags follow the same convention: [P] primary text read, [S] secondary, [I] inference, [U] not yet researched.

## What it is

The private workspace of **one advocate** (user zero, as the court's is one judicial officer): their clients, matters, diary, tasks, notes, time, documents and their side of a plea negotiation. It is not the court's record and never holds another party's records. It is a Tauri app on the same foundation as Courts: SQLCipher database with the OS-keystore key, one `/api/**` router, the workspace shell with tabs, a hash-chained audit trail from `packages/node-core` (`audit`, version 1), and a recovery screen. Dev API on 47924, Vite on 3009, keystore name `com.pleadli.lawyers`.

## What is built

- **Clients and matters.** A client has a name, contact and, for someone accused, custody (at liberty, remanded, serving) and where they are held. A matter has a title, the **advocate's own reference** (free text: the research found no Ugandan numbering standard and no formal meaning of "matter" [P/U], so none is invented), the court's number, court and level, offence, and **how the advocate came to act**: privately, appointed at the expense of the state, pro bono, or through a legal aid clinic. A closed matter takes nothing new until reopened; it cannot close with a hearing still listed.
- **The diary.** A hearing is listed, then closed as **attended** (what was done is required), **adjourned** (a structured reason and responsible party, the *same enumerations the court records*, so the two sides can describe an adjournment in the same words; a next date fixed at the time lists the next hearing), **missed** (recorded as it is, never hidden) or **cancelled**. A printable day view; an unclosed past hearing is an open item on Today.
- **The state-funded scheme's dated duties [P].** The Judicature (Legal Representation at the Expense of the State) Rules, 2022, Rule 13, as read in `RESEARCH.md` section 4: an appointed advocate must confirm a meeting with the accused to the court in writing within **2 working days**, and submit a **certificate of trial readiness**, completed jointly with the prosecutor, within **5 working days before the hearing**. For a state-appointed matter the application makes these two tasks with due days, and keeps the second in step with its hearing (moved when the hearing is moved, replaced when it is adjourned with a next date, dropped if the hearing is cancelled or missed). **Working days skip Saturday and Sunday; public holidays are not excluded** (no list is held), and the exact day the 2 days start from is read as the day of appointment: both are stated on the screens and should be checked against the Rules' own wording before anyone relies on the exact day. These rule tasks can be completed but not edited or removed.
- **Tasks, notes, time.** Manual tasks with a due day; notes on a matter; time in minutes (`90`, `1h30`, `45m`), totalled by matter. Time is only recorded: whether it is billed follows the engagement (private time is; state-funded work is paid by the Rules' schedule; pro bono is not, and the two tracks never pay together [P]), and **nothing is priced**.
- **Documents.** Bail application, submissions, list of authorities, letter, advice, other: written in the shared rich-text editor (saved as you write), then **filed**: a day, and with whom; it gets `DOC/<year>/<n>` and a SHA-256, and is frozen (database triggers). Filing needs the advocate's name in Settings. Whether Ugandan practice prescribes a form for any of these is **not settled** [U]; no prescribed form is asserted, and the page layout is a working template.
- **Plea negotiation, defence side.** The research found the plea agreement co-authored (prosecutor, accused and advocate sign), the confirmation before the court a separate step, and the duty of **explaining the agreement to the accused before they sign to fall on the advocate or a justice of the peace, not the prosecutor** [P, from the Plea Bargain Rules as read in `RESEARCH.md` section 3; the Rules' text was not re-read for this build]. So the record refuses "agreement reached" without when and by whom it was explained, refuses "confirmed" without signing and confirmation dates in order, and shows an agreed-but-unconfirmed negotiation on Today. It records **this side only**, and is matched to the DPP's and the court's records by court number when those connections exist.
- **Today** (the Police overview shape): banners, stat tiles, in court today with actions, the week ahead, needs attention (hearing not closed, no hearing listed, task overdue or due within 3 days, plea agreed and not confirmed), an 8-week adjournment trend and the reasons for them.

## Not built (deliberately, or not yet possible)

- **No connection to Courts, DPP or Prisons.** `packages/interchange` is today the Police to DPP contract (referrals, receipts, decision notices, pairing of a station with an office). Hearing notices, orders, judgments and the certified record from Courts, filings to the court, and the shared plea record are new message kinds and a new pairing shape that need their own design and both apps' code. Courts owns the record; Lawyers must never see another case's record, and "referenceable is not visible" (Courts proposal, section 7) is the rule the message design must keep.
- Public holidays, invoices and fee schedules, attachments and scanned documents, a bundled statute library (Courts has one you fill yourself; Lawyers does not yet), app lock, backups, roles.
- The Legal Aid Roll, appointment by rotation and the removal rules [P] are the Judiciary's side and are not modelled.

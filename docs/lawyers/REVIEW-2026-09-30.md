# Pleadli Lawyers: review, research and plan (2026-09-30)

The user's direction, in their words: the Lawyers app is "missing a lot". It is for everyone who practises law, not only defence: advocates, state attorneys, the Attorney General's side, and even a paralegal. It is personal by default ("just single, not necessarily needed to share") except that a lawyer downloads what they file and gives it to the court. It can be used alone or under a firm. The user picks what they use it for, can add more later, and never sees another kind of lawyer's tools unless they ask for them. Follow the DPP structure (country, jurisdiction, AI and so on). It needs more than law knowledge and quick actions: clients, filings, and making the everyday work simpler, with recommendations per scenario. Everything goes in one place.

This document is the review and the plan. **No code has been written for it.** Decisions D1 to D12 at the end need an answer, or an "accept the recommendations", before Phase L1 starts.

Evidence tags as elsewhere: [P] primary text read this session, [S] secondary, [I] inference, [U] not yet researched (backlog, not "out of the product").

## 2. Who uses it, and what the law lets each of them do

The law makes one distinction the product has to enforce: **only an advocate with a valid practising certificate may conduct proceedings or draw instruments for a fee.** Anyone else may use the app but not sign or file as counsel.

| Finding | Source | Tag |
|---|---|---|
| A non-advocate who acts as an advocate, issues process, or conducts a suit commits an offence, *except* under Law Council legal aid regulations | Advocates Act s.64(1)-(2) | [P] |
| A non-advocate may not, for a fee, draw instruments about property, legal proceedings, company formation or partnership. Exempt: public officers on duty, persons only engrossing (typing up), a company's full-time employee acting for that company. Wills and powers of attorney are not "instruments" here | s.66 | [P] |
| Every such instrument must be endorsed with the drawer's name and address; a registering authority may not accept it otherwise | s.67 | [P] |
| Every advocate keeps books showing each client's money separately from any other money, and keeps them 6 years | Advocates Act, First Schedule (Accounts Rules) r.9 | [P] |
| Breaking the Accounts Rules is professional misconduct and an offence | s.43 (s.40 requires compliance) | [P] |
| Practising certificate is annual, to 31 December; refused if the ULS subscription is unpaid | s.11, s.12(1)(e) | [P] (earlier research) |
| 40 pro bono hours a year | Pro Bono Regulations 2009 | [S] |
| Commissioner for oaths: an advocate of 2+ years, appointed by the Chief Justice. Notary: 5+ years' standing | Commissioners for Oaths (Advocates) Act; Notaries Public Act | [S] |
| Whether a commissioner or notary must keep a register of oaths/notarial acts | | [U] |
| Attorney General's chambers: Directorate of Civil Litigation (represents Government in courts and tribunals), Directorate of Legal Advisory Services (advice to Government); regional chambers opening (Masaka, Hoima, Arua) | justice.go.ug | [S] |
| Party-and-party costs and advocate-client charges follow scales (Schedules 1 to 6); since 2018 a pre-taxation meeting must list the agreed items first | Advocates (Remuneration and Taxation of Costs) Regulations, reg. 13A (SI 7 of 2018) | [P] |

So the people, and what each one can do in the app:

| Person | Can do | Cannot do (backend-refused) |
|---|---|---|
| **Advocate** (private practice, any area) | Everything in their chosen usages; sign, file, issue | |
| **State attorney** (AG chambers, a ministry, a local government) | Government suits, advice, contract vetting, their own diary and deadlines | Nothing refused: they are advocates acting as public officers (s.66(2)(a)) |
| **In-house counsel** | Company matters, contracts, board papers, outside counsel tracking | Nothing refused for the employer (s.66(2)(c)) |
| **Legal aid officer / clinic advocate** | Legal aid cases, intake, referral | |
| **Paralegal, clerk, legal assistant, student** | Intake, drafting, diary, tasks, filing logistics, time | Sign or file as counsel, issue a document under their own name, open a trust ledger payment. Their drafts go to a named advocate to sign (s.64, s.66, s.67). A paralegal working under legal aid regulations gets the legal aid usage (s.64(2)) |
| **Accounts person (firm)** | Client and office ledgers, invoices, receipts | Legal work |
| **Prosecutor** | See D4: the DPP app is their tool. Lawyers offers a "Prosecution" usage only for private prosecutions and prosecuting agencies outside the DPP | |

## 3. Research: the deadlines a lawyer lives by (Uganda)

These become **app-computed clocks**, the same principle as DPP's custody clocks: the app counts, Assist only explains.

| Clock | Rule | Source | Tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Serve summons | 21 days from issue (extension applied for within 15 days after) | CPR O.5 r.1(2) | [P] |
| File defence | By the day in the summons: 15 days after service | CPR O.9 r.1, summons form | [P] |
| Reply to defence | 15 days after the defence | CPR O.8 r.18(1) | [P] |
| Reply to counterclaim | 15 days after service | CPR O.8 r.11 | [P] |
| Pleadings close | 7 days after the reply | CPR O.8 r.18(5) | [P] |
| Amend plaint without leave | 21 days from filing, or 14 days from the defence | CPR O.6 r.20 | [P] |
| Summons for directions | Plaintiff takes out within 28 days of the last reply; returned within 14 days | CPR O.XIA r.1 (2019 amendment) | [P] |
| Scheduling conference | 7 days after the discovery order, or 28 days after the last reply | CPR O.12 r.1 | [P] |
| ADR | Finished within 21 days of the order (+15 on application) | CPR O.12 r.2 | [P] |
| Interlocutory applications | Cut-off 21 days after ADR (15 after scheduling if no ADR); serve 15 days; reply 15 days; hearing within 21 days; decided within 45 days | CPR O.12 r.3 | [P] |
| Interrogatories | Answered within 10 days | CPR O.10 | [P] |
| Notice of appeal (civil, to Court of Appeal) | 14 days after the decision, lodged with the High Court registrar | CoA Rules r.76(2) | [P] |
| Memorandum and record of appeal | 60 days after lodging the notice | CoA Rules r.83(1) | [P] |
| Notice of appeal (criminal, non-capital) | 14 days after the decision | CoA Rules r.60 | [P] |
| Suing Government, a local authority or scheduled corporation | 45 days' written notice first (Second Schedule form); the plaint must say it was given | CPLMPA s.2 | [P] (courts have since held it directory, not mandatory [S]) |
| Tort against Government | 2 years | CPLMPA s.3(1) | [P] |
| Contract against Government / local authority | 3 years | CPLMPA s.3(2) | [P] |
| Contract or tort (general) | 6 years; personal injury 3 years | Limitation Act s.3(1) | [P] |
| Recover land | 12 years | Limitation Act s.5 | [P] |
| Enforce a judgment | 12 years (interest 6) | Limitation Act s.3(3) | [P] |
| Penalty under an enactment | 2 years | Limitation Act s.3(4) | [P] |
| Disability | 12 months after it ends | CPLMPA s.5 | [P] |
| State brief duties | 2 working days / 5 working days before hearing | 2022 Rules r.13 | [P] (already built) |
| Practising certificate | Renew by 31 December | Advocates Act s.11 | [P] |
| Criminal custody limits | Reuse the DPP pack clocks (48h, remand, bail) | `@pleadli/setup` | [P] |

Not yet researched [U], so provisional in the pack and flagged on screen: High Court appeals from magistrates (civil), Supreme Court rules, the Employment Act and Industrial Court, Land Act and land tribunals, Succession Act and probate, Companies Act filings (URSB), tax appeals (TAT), the other East African countries' civil procedure. "Unknown" means backlog, not "not in the product".

Filing: ECCMIS lets advocates e-file documents and pay fees through a URA payment reference (PRN) [S, Judiciary pages read by `docs/courts/RESEARCH.md`]. It is live in a growing number of courts, not all of them [U]. **No ECCMIS integration is assumed.** Pleadli prepares exactly what gets uploaded or printed, and records what happened.

## 4. The recommended model: one app, many usages

### 4.1 The idea in one sentence

**One Pleadli Lawyers app. At setup you say who you are and what you do. The app shows only those tools. You add or remove usages later in Settings.** Everything shares one core (clients, matters, calendar, documents, money, Assist), and each usage adds its own matter types, stages, deadlines, templates and registers.

### 4.2 Setup (reusing `@pleadli/setup`, one new step)

Welcome, licence, **country** (the pack), then:

1. **Alone or a firm/office.** Alone: one person, no roster (today's model). A firm, chambers or an office: people and roles (Advocate, Paralegal/clerk, Accounts), same staff table and TOTP as DPP.
2. **Who you are:** Advocate in private practice, State attorney, In-house counsel, Legal aid provider, Paralegal/clerk. This decides which *actions* are allowed (section 2), not which screens exist.
3. **What you do** (tick any number; each shows one line of what it adds):

| Usage | Adds |
|---|---|
| Civil litigation | Civil suit and application types, pleadings stages, CPR clocks, plaint/defence/reply/affidavit/notice of motion templates, limitation checker |
| Criminal defence | Today's criminal build: custody, bail, plea negotiation, state brief duties, mitigation |
| Appeals | Notice of appeal (14d) and memorandum (60d) clocks, record of appeal checklist |
| Land and conveyancing | Transaction matters (due diligence, agreement, transfer, registration), sale agreement and transfer templates, conveyancing fee scale (reg. 13A 2018 scale) |
| Companies and commercial | Incorporation, annual returns, resolutions, contract review, company register of clients' companies |
| Family and succession | Divorce, custody, maintenance, letters of administration, probate, wills (a non-advocate may draft a will: s.66(3)) |
| Employment | Claims, labour office / Industrial Court stages [U clocks] |
| Advisory and opinions | Question, research, opinion, delivered; legal opinion template |
| Government (AG / state attorney) | Suits against and by Government, the 45-day notice, 2y/3y limits, advice requests from ministries, contract vetting |
| Legal aid | Intake and eligibility, referral, clinic statistics, pro bono hours |
| Commissioner for oaths / notary | Walk-in register (who, what document, when, fee), jurat text, notarial certificates |
| Prosecution (private / agency) | Only if D4 says so |

Settings > **Usages** is the same list, where a usage can be added later. Removing one hides it; the matters it created stay readable (nothing is deleted).

4. Look (colour, logo, letterhead), protection level, first users. Done.

### 4.3 The core every user gets

- **Today:** what is due (clocks first, red when late), in court today, tasks, unpaid invoices, walk-ins. Only for the usages switched on.
- **Matters:** board (stages per matter type) or list. A matter has a **type** from a usage, **parties** (client, other side, opposing counsel, witnesses, the court), stage, clocks, documents, money.
- **Conflict check** before a matter opens: every client and party name searched across all matters (a professional-conduct duty [S], and something no small practice does well on paper).
- **Clients:** people and organisations. Custody fields only appear for criminal clients.
- **Calendar:** hearings, clocks and tasks in one view (DPP calendar component).
- **Documents:** per-country templates with letterhead, signature, stamp, verify code and QR (DPP's Phase 3 engine). Drawer's name and address are printed automatically (s.67). Paralegal drafts show "to be signed by" and go to an advocate's queue.
- **Filing pack** (the "download and share with the court" ask): choose documents, get **one paginated PDF** (cover, index, documents, affidavit of service slot) sized for ECCMIS upload or printing, plus a **filing record**: court, date, ECCMIS/court reference, fee and PRN, who was served, when, proof of service. Filing a document freezes it (already built).
- **Money** (switchable, on by default for private practice):
  - **Client account ledger**, per client, kept apart from office money, 6 years (Accounts Rules r.9). The app refuses paying out more than a client's balance.
  - **Fee notes and invoices** from time, fixed fees or the scale; receipts; disbursements.
  - **Bill of costs** for taxation after judgment, laid out by the scale, with the 2018 "agreed items" column.
  - State brief and pro bono matters stay unbilled (already true).
- **Assist** (DPP's backend, per usage): draft a pleading or letter from the facts; a chronology from the papers; summarise a judgment or the other side's pleading; spot what the other side has not answered; explain a clock (never compute it); fill a template paragraph. It quotes only stored law with its "as at" date. Law library via Laws.Africa (Uganda consolidated statutes are CC-BY on ULII [P]).
- **Import papers:** photograph or scan what the client brings, and Assist splits, names and files it (DPP's importer).
- **Statistics:** matters by type and stage, clocks met and missed, fees billed and collected, pro bono hours.
- **Practice compliance** card in Settings: practising certificate expiry, ULS subscription, pro bono hours towards 40, commissioner or notary appointment.

### 4.4 Stages per matter type (the board columns)

| Type | Stages |
|---|---|
| Civil suit | Instructions, Pre-action (demand, 45-day notice), Pleadings, Directions and ADR, Trial, Judgment, Costs and execution, Closed |
| Application | Filed, Served, Reply, Hearing, Ruling, Closed |
| Criminal defence | Instructions, Custody and bail, Plea and disclosure, Trial, Sentence and mitigation, Appeal, Closed |
| Appeal | Notice lodged, Record requested, Memorandum filed, Hearing, Judgment, Closed |
| Transaction (land, company) | Instructions, Due diligence, Drafting, Signing, Registration, Completed |
| Succession | Instructions, Petition, Gazetting and caveats [U], Grant, Distribution, Closed |
| Advice / opinion | Question, Research, Opinion drafted, Delivered |
| Legal aid case | Intake, Eligibility, Advice or referral, Representation, Closed |

As in DPP: Assist can propose the next step for a stage, and a named person accepts it. It never moves a matter on by itself.

### 4.5 Sharing

- **Default: nothing leaves the computer.** One person, one encrypted database.
- **To the court:** the filing pack (PDF), printed or uploaded to ECCMIS by the user.
- **To the client:** a client copy (letter, invoice, document), saved, printed or emailed from the device.
- **Firm on one network:** the Prisons pinned-TLS LAN exchange (`packages/node-core`), one office machine holding the matters and others connecting. This is D6, not Phase L1.
- **To Courts/DPP apps:** later, through `packages/interchange`, using "referenceable is not visible" (Courts proposal section 7). Not planned yet.

## 6. Structure

Sidebar (same as the reworked DPP): **Today, Matters** (board / list / saved views), **Calendar, Clients, Documents, Money, Statistics**, then usage-owned places only when switched on (**Registers** for oaths and notary, **Intake** for legal aid). Ribbon: Home, Search, Add, Assist.

Matter workspace tabs: **Matter** (cover, stage stepper, clocks, parties), **The file** (every document, in order, with sheet numbers), **Money**, **Activity**.

Data (sketch, for Phase L1 design, not final):
- `installation`, `staff` (role: advocate / paralegal / accounts, practising certificate number and expiry), `usages` (enabled set)
- `matters` + `type`, `stage`, `usage`; `parties` (matter, role, person/organisation); `conflict_checks`
- `clocks` computed from pack rules plus `matter_events` (served on, defence filed, decision given), like DPP `custody_events`
- `filings` (document set, court, reference, fee/PRN, served, proof)
- `ledger_entries` (client account vs office, per client), `invoices`, `bills_of_costs`
- `register_entries` (oaths, notarial acts)
- the existing tables stay; criminal-only columns move behind the Criminal defence usage

Country: a **practice pack** beside the existing institution pack in `@pleadli/setup`: courts, clocks above, templates, fee scale reference, profession body (Uganda Law Society, Law Society of Kenya, Tanganyika Law Society, Rwanda Bar Association, Barreau in DRC/Burundi). Uganda first and sourced; others provisional and labelled.

## 8. Decisions needed before any code

| # | Decision | Recommendation |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | One app with usages, rather than separate apps per kind of lawyer | **One app, usages chosen at setup, changeable in Settings** |
| D2 | Existing criminal build | **Keep it all, as the "Criminal defence" usage.** Existing installs get Criminal defence switched on automatically |
| D3 | Alone vs firm | **Both at setup.** Alone = no roster (today). Firm = staff with roles (reusing DPP's staff/TOTP). This reverses the "single advocate" scope in spec 0001 |
| D4 | Prosecutors | **DPP prosecutors use Pleadli DPP.** Lawyers gets a "Prosecution" usage only for private prosecutions and prosecuting agencies outside the DPP [U: which agencies prosecute in their own name needs research]. Or: leave it out for now |
| D5 | Paralegal limits | **Backend-enforced:** no signing, filing, issuing or trust payments by non-advocates; drafts go to a named advocate (Advocates Act ss.64, 66, 67) |
| D6 | Firm sharing | **Later (L6)**, one office machine on the LAN like Prisons. Until then each person's install is their own |
| D7 | Clocks | **App-computed from the pack, never by AI**, like DPP. Unresearched ones shown as "provisional" |
| D8 | Court filing | **No ECCMIS integration assumed.** Pleadli makes the PDF pack and the record; the user uploads or prints |
| D9 | Money depth | **Client account ledger + invoices + bill of costs** (the law requires the ledger). No bank or mobile money integration yet |
| D10 | Oaths/notary register | **Build it** (it is useful even though whether a register is mandatory is [U]) |
| D11 | Countries | **Uganda first with sources**, the other nine as provisional packs, researched one by one later |
| D12 | Assist provider | **Same as DPP** (Rust backend, office's own key, providers Gemini / OpenRouter / Claude / OpenAI-compatible), shared code moved into a package rather than copied |

## 16. Relay step 4, part 1: the computer side in node-core (written 2026-11-18)

- **`packages/node-core/src/postbox.rs`**, behind the new cargo feature `postbox` (reqwest, serde_json, tokio; no new crates). Two tables to copy into an app's migration (`postbox::TABLE_SQL`): `postbox_outbox` and `postbox_inbox`.
- **It moves opaque bytes only.** Sealing and opening stay in `pleadli-interchange` (`seal`, `peek`, `open`), so a bug here cannot expose a case file. It checks only the SHA-256.
- **API:** `Postbox::new(pool, base_url)`; `queue(id, to, kind, bytes)` (idempotent on the package id); `flush(token)` posts what is due; `collect(token)` fetches, stores, THEN acknowledges (a crash in between re-fetches, nothing is lost; a hash mismatch is left at the relay); `refresh(token)` reads delivered/acked/expired from `outbox?ids=`; `unread()` and `handled(id)` for the app; `release_blocked(to)` after a connection is approved; `sent()` for the progress line; `tick(token)` does the whole round.
- **Rules as a pure function** (`classify`): 2xx done; 429 and 5xx retry (the relay's `retryAfterSeconds` honoured within 1 minute to 6 hours; else 5 min doubling to 6 h); 401/403 `blocked` (not_connected, licence_needed) until a person fixes it, then `release_blocked`; other 4xx `failed`. Unsent for 30 days becomes `expired`.

# Case 1: simple theft, magistrate court, accused has his own advocate, bail granted

Status: script written 2026-11-24. **The procedure below is from general knowledge of Uganda criminal practice and is NOT yet checked by a practitioner.** Anything marked (verify) must be confirmed before this case is trusted as proof. Names, stations, courts and numbers are fictional.

## What this case proves

The baseline chain Police, DPP, Court, Lawyer, in real windows, file route, with an adjournment, a bail application, and two things that must be refused. It does not cover custody-to-lawyer (case 2) or Prisons (bail is granted, so no remand).

## The offence

Theft of a motorcycle (UG reg. UFA 123X, value about UGX 4,500,000) from outside a market on 2026-11-10. Charge: theft contrary to sections 254(1) and 261 of the Penal Code Act (verify the section numbers and the current Cap). Triable by a magistrate grade one (verify).

## The procedure, step by step

1. **Police, complaint and arrest.** Akello reports the theft (CRB entry). The motorcycle is found with Opio on 2026-11-14 and he is arrested. In Police: open the case, record both people, the complainant's statement, the arrest and the custody record. He must be brought before a court within 48 hours (Constitution art. 23(4)(b), verify). He is released on police bond the same day (verify that police bond is the usual route for theft).
2. **Police, charge sheet and referral.** The investigating officer completes the charge sheet and refers the file to the DPP office. Sent as a sealed file to DPP.
3. **DPP, receive and decide.** The state attorney receives the referral, creates the prosecution file, reviews the statement and evidence, and sanctions the charge of theft. The receipt goes back to Police (Police sees "accepted").
4. **DPP, file to court.** DPP files the case at the Chief Magistrates Court (magistrate level). The receipt goes back to DPP.
5. **Court, register.** The registrar accepts the filing, which registers the case and gives it a court number. The number is fictional: "CR 301/2026".
6. **Lawyer, retained and connected.** The family tells Advocate Nabirye the court and the case number. The firm, already connected to this registry, opens a matter with that court number and the client Opio Samuel.
7. **Court, first sitting: plea.** The magistrate takes the plea. Opio pleads not guilty. Court adjourns for the bail application and tells the firm the date (notice of hearing).
8. **Lawyer, files a bail application** on the matter. The firm sees "filed" and waits.
9. **Court, decides.** The registrar accepts the application at the next sitting, the magistrate rules, bail is granted on terms (cash bail, two sureties, report monthly, verify). The matter adjourns to a hearing date and the firm gets the new notice.
10. **Lawyer, sees the result.** The firm's matter shows the filing "accepted" and the hearing in the diary.

## What must be refused (and who must understand why)

- **R1. A notice on the wrong matter.** The clerk tries to file the hearing notice for CR 301/2026 on the firm's other matter CR 305/2026. Refused, with the case number named.
- **R2. A notice from another court with the same number.** A second registry (Fictional High Court Registry) sends a notice numbered "CR 301/2026". The firm's matter belongs to the first court. Refused, naming both courts. (Built 2026-11-24.)
- **R3. Anything after the link is ended.** Not run in this case (it was run on 2026-11-24 in the earlier Courts and Lawyers run).

## What each person must be able to see

- Police: the referral shows accepted by the DPP office, with their registration number.
- DPP: the prosecution file shows filed at the court, with the court's case number.
- Court: the case, the sitting, the adjournment reason, the bail filing and the ruling.
- Lawyer: the matter with its court number, the hearing in the diary, the filing status, and a refusal in plain words when a notice does not belong.

# Case 2: held at a police station with no lawyer, inside the 48 hours

Status: script written 2026-11-24. Procedure from general knowledge, NOT checked by a practitioner. Fictional names.

## What this case proves

A person in custody is asked about a lawyer, the answer is recorded and stays visible while the 48-hour clock runs, and it changes when the person gets one. Design chosen by the owner: record only. Nothing is sent to a lawyer or legal aid body (so this case does not test any contact with Lawyers).

## The people

| Person | Role | App |
|---|---|---|
| Kato Ronald, 29 | Arrested on suspicion of theft of a motorcycle | Police |
| Cpl. (Test Officer) | Station officer | Police |

## Steps

1. **Police:** book Kato Ronald in, with the arrest time and the cell. The 48-hour deadline shows.
2. **Police:** on booking, record that he has been asked and has not yet answered ("Not yet asked" is shown as the lawyer status on the board).
3. **Police:** he asks for legal aid. Edit the record: Lawyer = Legal aid, with the name of the body. The board card shows "Lawyer: Legal aid, <body>".
4. **Police:** later his brother arranges an advocate. Edit: Lawyer = Own advocate, with the advocate's name. The board shows the new answer.
5. **Refusals the officer must see:** choosing a lawyer type and leaving the name empty is refused with "Write down who the lawyer or the legal aid body is."

## Must be true

- The lawyer status is visible where the 48-hour clock is, not buried in a note.
- A named lawyer or body is always required for the three named kinds.
- Nothing about it leaves the station (record only).

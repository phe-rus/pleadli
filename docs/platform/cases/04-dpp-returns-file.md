# Case 4: the DPP returns the file for more investigation

Status: script written 2026-11-24. Procedure from general knowledge, NOT checked by a practitioner (verify items marked). Fictional names.

## What this case proves

A refusal travelling back from the DPP to Police and being understood by the investigating officer: what was wrong, what to do, and that the file can come back. Police and DPP only.

## Steps

1. **Police:** the case CRB 452/2026, complainant statement, suspect statement, charge sheet, referral to DPP. No bank records, no witness from the church treasury.
2. **DPP:** accepts, opens a charging review. The evidential test fails: no proof the money was obtained by a false representation; no bank statement. The state attorney records a decision to **return for further investigation** (the decision type "return for investigation") with directions: get the bank statements, take the treasurer's statement, show the false pretence.
3. **DPP to Police:** the decision notice, with the directions, goes to Police.
4. **Police finds out:** the referral shows "returned" with the directions in words an officer can act on; the case shows open work (statements and records to get).
5. **Police acts:** takes the treasurer's statement and attaches the bank record, then **re-refers**. DPP sees it as a resubmission of the same file, not a new one, and reviews again.
6. **DPP:** the second review passes. Sanctions.

## Must be true

- The officer is told what to fix, not only "rejected".
- The re-referral is tied to the first (the DPP sees the history).
- The 48-hour and bond clocks are not affected by the return (verify in practice: a suspect on police bond has to be charged or released within the bond period).

# Case 7: civil commercial suit between two firms

Status: script written 2026-11-24. Procedure from general knowledge, NOT checked by a practitioner. Fictional names.

## What this case proves

The civil side of the platform with no Police, DPP or Prisons: plaint, summons, service, defence with a counterclaim, replies, directions, judgment. Courts and Lawyers only, with a firm on each side.

## The people

| Person | Role | App |
|---|---|---|
| Kampala Fresh Foods Ltd | Plaintiff, owed UGX 84,000,000 for produce | Lawyers (plaintiff's firm) |
| Lakeview Hotels Ltd | Defendant, says deliveries were spoiled and counterclaims | Lawyers (defendant's firm) |
| Registrar, Commercial Division | | Courts |

## Steps

1. Plaintiff's firm opens a civil suit matter, records when the cause of action arose, files the plaint, summons issued, summons served.
2. Court registers the suit and fixes a scheduling conference.
3. Defendant's firm enters its matter, records service, files a defence with a counterclaim.
4. Plaintiff's firm records the defence and counterclaim received; replies; summons for directions; the court sets a trial date; judgment.

## Status of the run (see `07-civil-commercial-suit-run.md`)

The court side cannot be run: Courts is criminal-only by recorded decision (D4 in `docs/courts/PROPOSAL.md`: "Criminal only for now, civil recorded as [U], not excluded"). A case in Courts has an accused, an offence and prosecutor roles, and no plaintiff, defendant or plaint.

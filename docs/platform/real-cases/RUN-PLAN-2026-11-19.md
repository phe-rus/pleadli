## Scenarios

1. **Murder, committed and tried** (Uganda v Kibuuka, `uganda-v-kibuuka-2025.md`): Police CRB, statements, post-mortem, custody > DPP file, indictment > Courts register (Kagadi committal then High Court session number) > sittings, remand order > Prisons records remand > judgment, sentence > warrant to Prisons, copy to Lawyers.
   Digital route: all five apps connected. Paper route: Police prints the file, DPP registers by hand, Courts registers by hand, Prisons receives a paper warrant.
2. **Theft, bail granted** (short case): Police charge > DPP sanction > Courts bail hearing > release order to Prisons > release recorded (never executes discharge).
3. **Charge withdrawn** (nolle prosequi): DPP withdraws > Courts closes > Prisons told to release.
4. **Mixed**: Police digital, DPP digital, Courts paper (the registry is not yet digital): DPP prints a filing sheet, court number typed back.

## Scenarios 2 and 3, real windows (2026-11-19, later)

**2. Bail granted (Kibuuka).** Courts issued a release order for bail and sent it to Hoima Government Prison. Prisons imported it, recorded it against the prisoner as an authenticated release order, and the receipt returned: Courts shows "recorded". Recording the order did NOT discharge him (status unchanged, by design); the officer then discharged him through `/api/prisons/releases` with the discharge gates. Seen working.

**3. Charge withdrawn.** New, built today: the prosecution tells the court a filed case is withdrawn (`case_withdrawal` package, nolle prosequi or discontinuance). DPP: "Withdraw a filed case" card and `POST /api/court/files/{id}/withdraw` (needs a court number and a trusted registry). Courts: the withdrawal appears in the DPP filings list with "Record the withdrawal"; recording concludes the case with the outcome ("Withdrawn by the prosecution (nolle prosequi): <reason>"), refused if a sitting is still scheduled or the number was never given by this registry; receipt (`withdrawal_recorded`) returns and the DPP shows "recorded the withdrawal". Round-trip tests on both sides. Run in the real windows on the Kibuuka case.

**4. Mixed route.** Covered in pieces: the DPP filing sheet (paper to the registry) plus the court registering by hand and the DPP typing the number back (CO 102/2026).

Also done: Police referral form warns when the case has no charge sheet (en, sw).

Still not done: Police and Prisons on the shared setup flow (they keep their own); Prisons reporting back to Courts; Lawyers filing to Courts; judgment and sentence to Prisons; a paper-arrival entry for police files; the native file dialogs (manual click-through owed); French wording not native-reviewed.

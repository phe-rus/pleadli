Purpose: do for Police what `docs/dpp/BENCHMARK-AND-GAPS-2026-11-26.md` did for the DPP. Say what the client complained about, compare the app with how a Ugandan station is documented to work, record what has been built and why, and rank what is left.

## 3. What has been done

### Before this session (kept, from AUDIT.md and the progress log)
Apple pass on the shell, Today, People (booking-sheet photographs, phone capture), Documents (papers and files), Station diary, Roster, Officers, Statistics, Settings; admin always has a password; test network of a station and a Command; sealed vault; hash-chained audit with a Settings screen; per-country rules (UG reviewed-pending, KE and DRC drafts) editable in-app with versions kept; papers carry the right force name; saves go to Downloads with no dialog; spoken statements; Police-to-DPP over `.pxpkg` and the postbox relay; custody lawyer access; referral status follows DPP decisions.

### This session (uncommitted, per the no-commit rule)
1. **One answer for "what does the station need next"** (`domain/attention.rs`, `GET /api/attention`). Before: the sidebar, Today, the folders, the command report and the phone alerts each decided for themselves. A case the DPP had accepted dropped back into Investigating. The custody limit was hard-coded to 48 h in two places that ignored the country rules. "Due soon" was 2, 6 or 12 hours depending on the screen. Now: one reading, derived from existing records, nothing stored. Folders, sidebar counts and custody figures all read it. It never says who a case is "assigned to", because no assignment exists in the records and PRODUCT.md leaves the assigned officer undecided.
2. **Police can read what the DPP now sends.** Migration `0046` lets a hearing minute be held until an officer matches it to a case (`needs_review`), the match is audited, and a restricted case the officer cannot open cannot be matched. `export_service_report` refuses names the minute never listed, requires a reason for a witness not served, and treats an identical resend as the same report.
3. **The screen for it** (`features/exchange/minutes-card.tsx`, on the DPP exchange page): "Hearing minutes" with Match to a case, then a tick per witness, a reason when not served, and Send by postbox or Save as file. English, French and Swahili. Typecheck passes.
4. **A test that broke the others** was fixed: my own attention tests set a process-wide "no sample data" variable, which made a different test (`attachments`) fail whenever the whole suite ran. That test now creates its own person. 41 unit tests pass, repeatedly.

**Not done and not seen:** none of items 1 to 3 has been looked at in the real window; per the owner's instruction only the typecheck was used today.

## 4. Proposal: six places, not twenty (needs owner approval before any change)

The DPP went from about 20 places to six (Today, Inbox, Files, Diary, Registry, Settings). Police has the same problem for the same reason: it was built from the paper registers, so every register became a place. PRODUCT.md says the sidebar "is a starting navigation, not the product definition", so this is allowed, but it changes names the client has seen, so it is a proposal.

| Place | What it holds | Replaces |
|---|---|---|
| Today | What needs the station now (one reading, section 3.1) | Today |
| Front desk | Station diary and incidents as one book, a complaint becomes a case from here | Station diary, Incidents |
| Cases | Folders Investigating, With the DPP, Closed, All. A case opens with its papers inside: statements, exhibits, charge sheet, referral, medical, the DPP's answer | Cases, Statements, Exhibits, Charge sheets, Referrals, Medical (the station-wide lists stay reachable through Search and Documents) |
| Cells | In custody and Police bonds | In custody, Bonds |
| People | People, with vehicles beside | People, Vehicles |
| Station | Officers, roster, armoury, impounded property, statistics, DPP exchange and postbox as one "Office of the DPP" page | the seven station places and two mail places |

Settings, Search and Documents stay as they are (not places). Risk: the officer who thinks "I want all exhibits" loses a door. Mitigation is Search and a Documents filter, and the owner's Apple test applies: one place per thing.

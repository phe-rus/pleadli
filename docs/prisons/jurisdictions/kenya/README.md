# Kenya jurisdiction pack, research page

Research only, priority 3 after Uganda and DR Congo. This is not an operational configuration.

Correction, 12 October 2026: the earlier claim that a Prisons and Correctional Services Act, 2024 is the starting point was not substantiated. It must not drive code. Start by obtaining the current official Prisons Act, Cap. 90, its amendments and subsidiary legislation, together with applicable judgments and the Criminal Procedure Code. The [Kenya Law ruling in Gachingiri v Republic, 11 March 2024](https://new.kenyalaw.org/akn/ke/judgment/kehc/2024/2735) refers to section 46 of Cap. 90. That reference is a research lead, not a complete present-day legal validation.

Kenya needs its own instruments, sentence and remand procedures, credit for time in custody, remission eligibility and exceptions, release authority, registers and calendar. Do not reuse Uganda's constitutional thresholds or section numbers.

The evidence rule is in [JURISDICTIONS.md](../../JURISDICTIONS.md). A pack leaves research only when its applicable statutes, source editions and rules are read, cited and verified with acceptance cases.

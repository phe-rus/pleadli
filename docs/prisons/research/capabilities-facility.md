# Pleadli Prisons — Facility Capability Model

> **Superseded 2026-09-18 by [`../product/02-capabilities-domains-workflows.md`](../product/02-capabilities-domains-workflows.md).** Evidence labels and statutory grounding here are reused. `NOT-BUILDABLE` items B3, B6, A11 and D4 are reclassified `NOT YET RESEARCHED (FIELD)` and modelled as provisional.

What one installation (one prison, one Officer in Charge) is grounded to *do*, grouped into capability areas.

**Evidence labels** (same convention as `findings.md`/`../PRODUCT.md`):
- **[Primary]** — authoritatively evidenced (Act text read in full, or UPS's own published documents/statistics).
- **[Secondary]** — credible institutional/press source, not independently confirmed.
- **[Product inference]** — clearly documented product inference from the above (labelled as such).
- **UNKNOWN / Needs UPS validation** — genuinely not evidenced; do not build.

**Build classification** per capability: **BUILD** (real, grounded, actionable now) · **DECISION** (grounded enough to require a product decision, not invention) · **RECORD-ONLY** (real but too thin for a feature; keep as a record field/list, not a domain) · **NOT-BUILDABLE** (UNKNOWN or explicitly not grounded).
